About us

Staples you can plan around.

Retailers and distributors need the same grain, the same grade and the same bag every time. We work with growers and mills to supply rice, sugar, jaggery and pulses, then clean, grade and pack them to the specification you ask for.

Our own brand, Hampi, takes that process one step further and arrives ready for the shelf.

Buyer FAQ

Before you order.

How do I start?

Send the product, grade and quantity you need through the form below. We reply with what we can supply and the terms.

Can I ask for a sample?

Mention it in your message and we will tell you how we handle samples for that product.

Can you pack to my bag size?

Yes. Tell us the bag size and labelling and we pack to it.

Do you pack under a private label?

Ask us in the form. Our own brand is Hampi, and we can discuss other arrangements.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. Give us your destination and we arrange freight and paperwork.

Will I know where my order is?

Your contact keeps you updated from dispatch until the goods arrive.
