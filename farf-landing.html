FAQ

Quick answers.

How much is delivery?

The delivery fee and the free-delivery threshold are shown in your cart before you pay.

How long does it take?

The delivery window is on every product page and in the Terms and returns page.

Can I return something?

Yes, unused items can be returned within the return window shown in the Terms and returns page.

What if an item arrives damaged?

Contact us with your order number and we will arrange a replacement or refund.

How do I pay?

Checkout runs through Stripe. You enter your address and card details on the Stripe page.

Can I order on WhatsApp?

Where it is offered, the cart has an Order on WhatsApp button.
